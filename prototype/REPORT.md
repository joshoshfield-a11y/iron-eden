## What was built (gameplay)

- **Player mech:** WASD/arrows with mass/momentum (acceleration + drag), mouse aim projected to the ground plane, hold-LMB fire. Weapons: **[1] Autocannon** (fast, 9 dmg, 3.2 heat/shot) and **[2] Railgun** (slow, 46 dmg, pierces, 17 heat/shot). **Space** dash (2.5 s cooldown). **Heat:** firing builds heat; passive cooling, **R** vents fast; at 100% you overheat — weapons disabled until heat falls below 30%.
- **Touch:** left-half virtual joystick moves; right-half touch fires with nearest-enemy aim assist.
- **Core Reactor** at center, 500 HP — lose it and the facility falls.
- **Build-anywhere deployables** (near player, facing point; keys or HUD buttons): **F** Autocannon Turret 50, **G** Generator 75, **B** Barricade 25 (160 HP blocker enemies must chew through). Collision + too-close-to-core checks.
- **POWER RULE:** turrets fire only when powered — within 28 m of the Core OR within 22 m of a live Generator. Faint power-radius rings are drawn (core cyan, generator gold). Kill the generator and its turrets go dark.
- **Enemies:** Scout (fast, core-bound), Heavy (slow tank, **directional armor** — 35% damage from the front arc, 150% from the rear via facing·hit-direction; below 50% HP it takes component damage: targeting/locomotion, slows, announced once), Burrower (wave 3 — spawns *inside* the perimeter near power structures and hunts generators/turrets first). Enemies attack the Core in range, and the player when very close.
- **Disable-vs-destroy salvage:** any enemy knocked to ≤15% HP becomes DISABLED (stops, greys, sparks) for 4 s. Press **E** next to it to CAPTURE it for intact salvage at 2.5× normal (+150% bonus). Otherwise it explodes for normal scrap.
- **Waves: exactly 3, then WIN.** W1: 8 scouts. W2: 6 scouts + 3 heavies. W3: 5 scouts + 3 heavies + 4 burrowers + **1 Siege Platform** (8× Heavy HP, crawls to 38 m and shells the Core). Intermission salvage bonuses.
- **Persistent consequence (WIN):** Core ≥75% → "Facility saved intact — becomes a Forward Base"; 1–74% → "Damaged Outpost + recovery contract"; core or player death → "Facility lost — Ruin" (game-over panel). **Mech Memory:** the win screen names the weapon that killed the Siege Platform — "*Autocannon/Railgun earned the mark: SIEGEBREAKER*". State is written to `user://iron_eden_world.json` (facility_state, core HP %, siege killer weapon, timestamp); an existing file is read on boot without crashing.
- **Substation choice:** at the NW substation, press **E** once — (A) power the 2 pre-placed dead turrets for free, or (B) open the sealed maintenance tunnel gate + 40 salvage cache. One choice, mutually exclusive.
